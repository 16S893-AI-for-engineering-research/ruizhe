PLANNING TIMELINE

From calibration to transfer.

STATUS / PROPOSED

Eleven weeks of evaluation will turn overpass-level predictions into calibrated plant and annual estimates, followed by a limited global transfer test.

WEEKS 1–3Step 01

DECOMPOSE

Error and sampling curves.

Separate random overpass noise from plant-specific bias, and quantify how satellite sampling differs from the true annual mean.

WEEKS 3–6Step 02

CALIBRATE

Cross-validation and intervals.

Run five-fold plant-level evaluation, calibrate conformal uncertainty, and derive the required overpass count.

WEEKS 6–9Step 03

EXPLAIN

Drivers and annual estimates.

Build the operating envelope, evaluate annual estimators, and test sensitivity to plant, scene, and sampling conditions.

WEEKS 9–11Step 04

TRANSFER

2018 test and global pilot.

Validate the early TROPOMI period over the United States, test one region abroad, and prepare the final report.
