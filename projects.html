PROJECT OVERVIEW

Measuring power plants from orbit.

Most power plants are not measured directly. U.S. plants report hourly emissions through continuous emissions monitoring systems (CEMS), but inventories elsewhere rely largely on fuel statistics and assumed emission factors.

TROPOMI maps nitrogen dioxide (NO2), the visible form of NOx, at a resolution fine enough to observe the plume drifting downwind of a large power plant.

Our pipeline finds these plumes, attributes them to source plants, and uses a convolutional neural network to estimate hourly NOx emissions from the NO2 field, wind, cloud, viewing geometry, and surface data.

RELIABILITY & LIMITS

How many overpasses are enough?

A satellite estimate contains random error that shrinks as observations accumulate and systematic error that averaging cannot remove.

Separate noise from bias.

Estimate both error components from held-out residuals for small, medium, and large emitters.

METHOD 02Calibrate

UNCERTAINTY

Build plant-level intervals.

Use conformal prediction at the plant level so shared plant bias is represented correctly.

03

OPERATING ENVELOPE

Which plants work best—and why?

Five-fold plant-level cross-validation will expand the well-observed held-out cohort from 7 to 46 plants. Every prediction will come from a model that never saw that plant during training.

01PLANT

Source properties

Capacity, fuel, emission controls, operating consistency, and distance from nearby sources.

02SCENE

Observation conditions

Wind, cloud, sun angle, surface brightness, aerosols, and viewing geometry.

03SAMPLE

Temporal coverage

Overpass count, timing, and clustering across seasons and years.

04MODEL

Learned behavior

Channel ablations and wind rotations will test whether the network uses physically meaningful signals.

DELIVERABLE

An operating envelope showing where emission magnitude and overpass count support estimates below a chosen error threshold.

FROM HOURS TO YEARS

Can sparse scenes support annual totals?

TROPOMI observes near 13:30 local time, only clear skies yield retrievals, and only a running plant produces a plume. The observed hours may not represent the full year.

Average sampled overpasses.

EST. 02Detect

NON-DETECTION

Use scenes without a plume.

PORTABLE

Correct sampling with global signals.

Use quantities available anywhere, such as plume-detection frequency.

EST. 04Pool

ACROSS YEARS

Borrow strength over time.

Stabilize years with few overpasses using observations from the same plant in other years.

TRANSFER TEST

A global pilot for 2018.

The pilot tests the method where hourly CEMS does not exist—without claiming a full global catalog. The United States provides a ground-truthed test of 2018 conditions before transfer abroad.

01COVERAGE
